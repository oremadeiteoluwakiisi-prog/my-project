Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can IoT sensors be deployed on our fields?

Most starter kits ship within 48 hours and can be self-installed in under 15 minutes per solar node. Our professional field technicians are also available for complete turnkey multi-thousand acre deployments.

Do Anti sensors require cellular cell tower coverage?

No. Our sensor probes utilize long-range LoRaWAN wireless protocol transmitting up to 15 kilometers (9.3 miles) to a single central solar gateway that connects via satellite or cellular backup.

Who owns the agronomy and yield data collected?

You do. 100% of field telemetry, yield maps, and soil data remain strictly your private property. Anti never shares or sells grower data to third parties.
